Why does changing the angle change the width?

For a fixed vertical rise, a flatter panel extends farther sideways. Both the base and the outer edges of the reflectors must fit in the 30 cm cube. The cup, handles, and fasteners count too. This simplified drawing omits fastener thickness, so leave a real construction margin.

What must stay clear?

The 22 October official clarifications allow lid/door and device-position handling inherent in cup insertion; opening a lid is part of cup insertion/removal. This model shows a fixed opening as a prototype choice, not a rule requiring one. No unrelated adjustments are permitted during heating. On removal, the cup must separate from every device component before the final temperature is measured. The opening check here only tests mouth width; it cannot check your hand grip, cup taper, friction, or real construction.

The plan-view 15 cm circle concerns protruding parts of the supervisor's lamp stand. It is not an extra dimension for the team's device. The exact stand may differ from this example.
