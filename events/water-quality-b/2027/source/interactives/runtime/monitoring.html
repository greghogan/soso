Design the next measurements

Repeat stations before and after rain and across the daily oxygen cycle. Add replicates, flow and source information, nutrient forms and biological observations. Algal growth and decomposition can connect nutrient inputs and oxygen demand, but these observations alone neither prove the source nor measure reef mortality. Compare context-appropriate baselines rather than a universal safe/unsafe cutoff.
