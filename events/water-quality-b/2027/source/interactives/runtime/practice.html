Water Quality B

Reef identification practice

Describe two diagnostic features before revealing the name. These examples teach recognition; the pool includes different species, views and life stages, but is not exhaustive. Give the event group rather than guessing an exact species when the evidence is insufficient. Image captions appear after reveal so they do not give away the target.

For a text-based alternative, choose described-image study. The description may name the organism; use it to explain which features support its identification rather than guessing its name.
